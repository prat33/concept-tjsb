The bank in figures

From one branch in Thane to a multi-state bank

The first branch of Thane Janata Sahakari Bank opened in 1972. Five decades on, TJSB Sahakari Bank Ltd is one of the country’s larger multi-state scheduled co-operative banks — still governed by its members, still audited and supervised under the co-operative framework.

Figures as published by the bank under “Milestones”. Its audited-financials creative for 2025–26 instead gives 165 branches across seven states as at 31 March 2026, and adds advances of ₹9,116 Cr, owned funds of ₹1,773 Cr, gross NPA 3.11%, net NPA 0.00% and CRAR 16.89%. Both branch counts are the bank's own; neither has been adjusted here.
